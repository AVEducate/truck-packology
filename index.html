AV EDUCATE · HOW TO PLAY

WHAT IS TRUCK PACKOLOGY?

Truck Packology teaches the logic AV crews use to load a show truck. Build a heavy base at the nose, pack high and tight, leave no gaps that let cases slide, secure every tier, and load in reverse of the venue's load-in order so the first gear needed comes off first.

A truck can be full and still be a bad pack. Every run is scored on space, stability, unload order and securement, and the crew lead explains each call. 30 beginner floor levels, 30 intermediate stacking levels, expert levels, offloading, and a final exam for your AV Educate certificate.

TOUCH
DRAGDrag a case down from the strip into the truck. Let go to set it down. Drag a case in the truck to move it. TWISTTwo-finger twist on the truck rotates a case 90°. DOUBLE-TAPTakes a case (or a bar or strap) back out. A case taken out counts as a rehandle. DRAG FLOORDrag empty truck floor up or down to scroll a long truck. BAR · STRAPDrag the button into the truck and let go behind a finished tier. VIEWFlips between the top view and the door view on stacked levels. ROTATETurns the case you're holding, or the one you just set down. A two-finger twist works too. BOTTOM BARTap or pull up the crew bar at the bottom for the level goal and case info. ≡ MENUHint, reset, help and the level list.
HOW SCORING WORKS
SCOREEvery pack is scored out of 100 on space (30), stability (25), unload order (20) and securement (15). A level only counts the parts it uses, so early levels score on space and stability. REHANDLESEvery case you pull back out after loading is 1 point off, up to 10. No rehandles is the only way to a perfect 100. PASSScore 60 or better and follow the level's lesson. Break the lesson and the level fails, even with a good score. The score still shows so you can see how the pack did. STARS100 ★★★ 95 to 99 ★★★ 80 to 94 ★★★
